Executive Summary: Across both listing periods (284 cumulative Days on Market: 223 days during the prior listing cycle and 61 days since re-launching on August 21, 2026), 234 Washington Avenue, Unit A has generated substantial online exposure (12,311 total listing views and 221,906 opened email campaigns) and steady physical tour activity, including multiple second and third visits from qualified buyers. 03 — Buyer Sentiment & Feedback Prospect Objections & Complete Showing Log (Prior & Current Listings)

  • 1. Recurring Interior Odor Issue (Flagged in Both April & September)

    During the previous listing cycle (April 13, 2026), an agent previewing the property noted a slight odor in the bedroom area. During the current relisting (September 14, 2026), our highest-intent prospect—who loved the home on August 31 and returned for a third showing with his wife while considering an offer—explicitly decided not to proceed due to concerns about the odor throughout the home.

  • 2. Multi-Level / Split-Level Layout & Ceiling Heights vs. Single-Level Condos

    Across both listing cycles, multiple second-home and local buyers praised the townhome's design, privacy, entrance, and two covered parking spaces, but hesitated over the vertical layout. In April, a family relocating from Hawaii felt the split levels were too much for young children (preferring at most two levels). In September, separate prospective buyers noted that the ceilings felt low in certain areas, leaned toward single-level high-rise condos instead, or remained unsure whether the multi-level space worked for their lifestyle.

  • 3. Prolonged A/C Mechanical Issue Stalling October Showings

    A scheduled private showing on October 5 had to be postponed due to an A/C malfunction. Because the A/C repair remained pending across the next three weeks (October 5 – October 25), no private showings could be conducted during the entire month of October, interrupting momentum after a busy September.

  • 4. Pricing & Amenity Comparison Against South of Fifth Condos

    At $3,500,000 ($1,421/SqFt) with a $2,664/month HOA fee and no resort amenities, buyers in the $2.5M–$3.5M bracket are weighing the townhome against full-service South of Fifth condominiums (such as Portofino Tower, where 2,340 SqFt 3-bedroom residences with full resort amenities have recently closed between $2,150,000 and $3,100,000, or $919–$1,325/SqFt) and nearby South of Fifth townhomes closed between $1,025,000 and $1,450,000 ($727–$1,142/SqFt).
