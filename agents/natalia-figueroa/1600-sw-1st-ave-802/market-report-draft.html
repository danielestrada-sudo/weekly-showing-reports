03 — Buyer Sentiment & Feedback Analysis Prospect Commentary & Complete Chronological Showing Log

  • 1. Unit Size & Spatial Perception for the $625,000 Price Point

    Buyers touring in September noted that while they appreciated the modern finishes and boutique atmosphere of Le Parc, the 962 sq. ft. interior felt compact for a primary 2-bedroom residence at $625,000. In particular, prospects comparing against slightly larger 1,000+ sq. ft. residences in the broader Brickell market felt they needed more living/entertaining space.

  • 2. Floor Level & View Expectations vs. Competing Inventory

    One private tour prospect specifically commented that they were seeking a different view orientation or higher floor in the building (Le Parc has active 2-bedroom listings on the 11th and 12th floors). At the current asking price, buyers are evaluating whether an 8th-floor line offers sufficient view premium compared to units priced under $550k on lower/mid floors.

  • 3. Complete Showing Lull Across October (Zero In-Person Tours)

    Following the 3 tours in September, zero showing appointments were booked between October 5 and October 25. While digital email reach remained robust (+8,229 opened campaigns per week), the complete absence of physical tour requests indicates that active Brickell buyers have filtered out the unit at $625,000 in favor of units priced closer to the $500k–$550k closed comp band.

  • 4. Strong Appreciation for Two Parking Spaces & Dual Storage Units

    Cooperating agents consistently praised the inclusion of two side-by-side second-floor parking spaces and two air-conditioned storage units—an exceedingly rare package in Brickell where extra parking spaces sell for $35,000+ and storage units rent for $150+/month. This remains our strongest competitive advantage to emphasize in future repositioning.
