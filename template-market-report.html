03 — Buyer Sentiment & Feedback Prospect Objections

  • Building Management & Amenities

    Recent buyers (as of late September) have explicitly stated they had unpleasant experiences with building management, causing them to lose interest. Additionally, prior prospects noted concerns regarding ongoing construction work and the temporary closure of the pools and balconies.

  • Condition vs. Value (Overpriced)

    Interested buyers, including those who already reside in the building, feel the unit is overpriced considering the amount of interior work and renovations required to meet their standards.

  • Appraisal & Sales Contingencies

    Serious buyers have indicated that any offer would be heavily contingent on comparable sales to support the appraisal. With the building's Average Closed Sale Price currently at $460/SqFt - substantially lower than our $582/SqFt list price - buyers and their agents recognize a significant appraisal risk. Furthermore, highly motivated buyers are requesting contingencies to sell their current properties before moving forward.
