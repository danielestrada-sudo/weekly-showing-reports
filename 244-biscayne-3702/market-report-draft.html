03 — Buyer Sentiment & Feedback Prospect Objections

  • Building Management & Amenities

    Recent buyers (as of late September) have explicitly stated they had unpleasant experiences with building management, causing them to lose interest. Additionally, prior prospects noted concerns regarding ongoing construction work and the temporary closure of the pools and balconies.

  • Condition vs. Value (Overpriced)

    Interested buyers, including those who already reside in the building, feel the unit is overpriced considering the amount of interior work and renovations required to meet their standards.

  • Appraisal & Sales Contingencies

    Serious buyers have indicated that any offer would be heavily contingent on comparable sales to support the appraisal. With the building's Average Closed Sale Price currently at $460/SqFt - substantially lower than our $582/SqFt list price - buyers and their agents recognize a significant appraisal risk. Furthermore, highly motivated buyers are requesting contingencies to sell their current properties before moving forward.

07 — Executive Decision Actionable Adjustments

Strategic AI Recommendations

  • Strategic Price Improvement: Recommend an immediate price reduction to the $599,000 - $615,000 range. This aligns closer to the building's $509/SqFt average, mitigating buyers' "appraisal fears" and accounting for the renovation costs repeatedly cited in recent feedback.
  • Renovation Credits / Incentives: Consider proactively offering a seller credit at closing (e.g., $10,000 - $15,000) explicitly earmarked for "Interior Updates" or "HOA Coverage." This psychologically validates the buyers' feedback about needed work and construction inconveniences without dropping the top-line price too aggressively.
  • Management & Showing Experience: Given the September 21st feedback regarding unpleasant experiences with building management, agents must preemptively brief prospective buyers about the building's strict protocols, smoothing out the entry process to prevent friction before they even see the unit.
  • Targeted Marketing: Continue focusing on investors and local buyers (like the South Tower residents) who are already familiar with the building's long-term value, as they are most likely to overlook short-term construction hurdles.

Based on the appraisal risks and current market saturation, an immediate strategic price adjustment is recommended to secure a closed contract before further momentum is lost.
