  • 2. Compact 932 SqFt Footprint, Kitchen Size & Interior Finishes vs. $595/SqFt Ask

    Across five separate weeks (Sept 21–27, Oct 12–18, Oct 19–25, Oct 26–Nov 1, and Nov 2–8), buyers felt the 932 SqFt floor plan and kitchen were smaller than expected in person (a deal-breaker for the Oct 19 Monday buyer), while the Nov 2–8 buyer passed in favor of a more modern, renovated, turnkey interior.

  • 3. Exterior-Style Building Hallways Without Air Conditioning (Weeks 4, 6 & 7)

    Several buyers objected to Gables Park Tower's un-air-conditioned exterior catwalk hallways (Oct 5–11, Oct 19–25, Oct 26–Nov 1), feeling the corridor appearance was less welcoming on arrival—even when they enjoyed the pool courtyard and Downtown Coral Gables location.

  • 4. $1,253/Mo Combined HOA/Assessment Cost & Warm Port St. Lucie Lead (Week 9)

    HOA fees ($1,000/month) and the $253/month special assessment caused multiple early showing cancellations and repeated buyer scrutiny. Positively, during Nov 9–15 a couple relocating from Port St. Lucie back to Coral Gables toured and specifically requested the full HOA documents and special assessment details, creating our most actionable current follow-up lead.

Recommended Next Steps to Capture a Contract

  • 1. Decisive Price Repositioning Below Unit #1507 & Into the `-06` Stack Sweet Spot ($525,000 – $529,000 | $563 – $568/SqFt): At $555,000, Unit #606 currently acts as a foil that sells Unit #1507 ($560,000 for 91 extra SqFt + den + 15th floor).   • 2. Offer a Seller Closing Credit to Pay Off the $253/Month Special Assessment Balance: Because buyers repeatedly raised concerns about the combined $1,253/month carrying cost ($1,000 HOA + $253 assessment), structure our marketing around a Seller Credit at Closing to Satisfy the Special Assessment (or cover 12–24 months of assessment payments). Neutralizing the assessment eliminates the #1 monthly affordability objection without forcing a steeper headline price cut.
  • 3. Direct Re-Engagement & HOA Value Comparison for the Port St. Lucie Relocating Couple: Follow up immediately with the Port St. Lucie buyers who requested HOA and special assessment documents during Nov 9–15 (plus the Saturday Oct 24 buyer who asked about HOA inclusions). Provide a clean side-by-side sheet highlighting that Gables Park Tower's $1,000/mo HOA (24/7 concierge/doorman, water, sewer, trash, building insurance, gym, pool & track) is $273–$710/month lower than nearby older Gables Laroc ($1,273–$1,710/mo), and emphasize Unit #606's <2-year-old A/C, refrigerator, washer/dryer, and impact glass.
  • 4. Cosmetic Modernization Touches to Counter the "Move-In-Ready" Feedback: Since the Week 8 buyer passed in search of a more modern look, minor high-ROI staging/refresh steps (neutralizing any dated light fixtures, decluttering countertops to visually expand the kitchen, and maintaining bright, crisp showing lighting prior to entry from the open-air corridor) will make the 932 SqFt layout photograph and show noticeably larger.
